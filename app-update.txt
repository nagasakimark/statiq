Faster, more reliable file opening

- Fixed: opening a file sometimes dropped you back on Home instead of opening it
- Files open much faster: the editor now loads while your file is being prepared, instead of one after the other
- Large presentations are prepared up to 10× quicker before opening
- The file converter warms up in the background on the Start screen
- Editing stays smoother: drafts are saved in the background less often
- Pictures pasted into Office as enhanced metafiles now show up
- New setting: "Compress large photos when opening" for photo-heavy presentations
